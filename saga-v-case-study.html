Saga V: coding the ritual behind a tarot-inspired mocktail brand

The Fool - Beginning

The Brief

Saga V is a mocktail brand built around a simple idea: every flavor is paired with its own tarot card, turning each drink into a small collectible ritual rather than just a beverage. The creative brief asked our team to bring the product line and its brand storytelling to life online.

As the web development team, our job was to give the brand a user-friendly e-commerce platform for visibility and customer acquisition - coding fully-functional, responsive prototypes from the UI/UX team's Figma designs, and matching the digital identity to Saga V's high-end packaging and storytelling vision.

Scope note -> this case study covers the About Us page specifically, since that's the place I built end-to-end. The rest of the site (home, recipe blog, shop, cart, checkout) was split across the rest of the web dev team from the same design system.

The High Priestess - Direction

Narrowing Three Concepts to One

Every team member took part in early concept brainstorming, and three ideas stood out enough to develop into sketches. Cross-team feedback on how each would fit the broader project cut two of them:

Velvet Hour

Conflicted with the Saga V tarot theme the brand team had already committed to.

Build-Your-Own Recipe

A custom mocktail recipe builder conflicted with the brand team's fixed drink selection.

Product Detail Modal

The concept we carried forward - a focused product quick-view used across the shop experience.

Design direction as a whole was led by the brand team, which kept the dev team's job clear: build what design signed off on, flag what didn't translate to the browser, and stay out of decisions that weren't ours to make.
